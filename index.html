About

An engineering workshop in Sofia

Aircom started as an aviation project and grew into a full-capability engineering workshop. We design, prototype, and manufacture — from aircraft structures to CNC-machined components to custom trailers. Every project gets the same precision.

What we do

Three areas of focus

Each one draws on the same engineering discipline. The aircraft work made us precise; we bring that standard to everything.

✈

Aviation Engineering

Design and construction of ultralight aircraft. Structural analysis, aerodynamics, flight testing. Our AcroCruiser F11AC is the result of years of iterative development.

▱

3D CAD & Design

Technical drawings, 3D modelling, and CAM programming for CNC production. From concept to manufacturing-ready files.

⚙

Custom Manufacturing

CNC milling, cutting, bending, engraving. Composite, metal, and wood structures. Motorcycle and custom trailers. If it can be engineered, we can build it.

Flagship

The AcroCruiser F11AC

A two-seat ultralight kit aircraft designed and built entirely in our workshop. Tandem seating, aerobatic-capable, with a FastBuild option that brings assembly down to 400-600 hours.

The F11AC is available as a complete kit or individual components. Everything from the composite fuselage to the HOTAS control system is engineered in-house.
