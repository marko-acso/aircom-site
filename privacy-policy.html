Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — clearly, and without the wall of legalese.

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Aircom Ltd. · EIK 200538540

The short version: we collect only what we need to respond to you or fulfil a request. We do not sell your data. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Who we are

Aircom Ltd. is an aviation engineering and manufacturing company registered in Bulgaria (EIK 200538540), with offices at ul. Okolovrasten pat 251, 621 vh.1 et.3, BG-1715 Sofia. We operate the website aircom.bg.

Questions about this policy? Write to us at info@aircom.bg — a real person will read it.

What we collect

We collect information only in specific situations:

  • Contact form — your name, email address, and message. We use this to reply to you.
  • Image upload form — photos or files you voluntarily submit under our Image Use Agreement, along with your name and email so we can credit you and stay in touch.
  • Analytics — we use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with the site through session recordings, heatmaps, and aggregated usage data (pages visited, clicks, scroll depth, browser type). Clarity is loaded only after you consent via our cookie banner. No individual user profiles are built for marketing purposes.

We do not collect payment information directly. If payment processing is ever added, it will be handled by a certified third-party provider.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries or requests
  • To fulfil the Image Use Agreement you entered into with us
  • To understand how the website is being used so we can improve it
  • To comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do not use your data for automated decision-making or profiling, and we do not send marketing emails unless you have specifically asked us to.

Cookies

This website uses a minimal set of cookies — no advertising trackers, no social media pixels. Analytics cookies are loaded only after you give consent via the cookie banner.

  • Session / functional cookies — kept briefly to make the site work correctly (e.g. form submissions).
  • Consent preference — we store your cookie consent choice in your browser's local storage (cookie_consent) so you are not asked again on every visit.
  • Analytics cookies (consent-gated) — set by Microsoft Clarity only after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. These help us see which pages are useful, how visitors navigate the site, and where we can improve. They do not identify you personally.
Cookie Provider Purpose Duration
_clck Microsoft Clarity Stores a unique user ID for analytics; persists the Clarity user identifier 12 months
_clsk Microsoft Clarity Connects page views into a single session for session replay and heatmaps 1 day
CLID Microsoft Clarity Identifies the first-time Clarity saw a user on any Clarity-enabled site 12 months

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site will continue to work, though some minor features may behave differently. You can also change your consent choice by clearing your browser's local storage for this site.

Who we share data with

We do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone. We may share information only with:

  • Our hosting provider, who stores files on our behalf under a data processing agreement
  • Microsoft Clarity (analytics), receiving session recordings, heatmaps, and aggregated usage data — only after you consent. Microsoft's privacy policy applies to data processed by Clarity.
  • Authorities, if we are required to by Bulgarian or EU law

Data retention

Contact form messages are kept for as long as needed to resolve your enquiry, typically no longer than 2 years. Uploaded images are kept for the duration of the Image Use Agreement, after which they are deleted unless you have agreed otherwise in writing. Analytics data is retained in aggregated form only.

You can ask us to delete your data at any time — see your rights below.

Your rights (GDPR)

Because we operate within the EU, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:

Access You can request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
Rectification If something is incorrect, you can ask us to fix it.
Deletion You can request that we delete your data at any time, no questions asked.
Portability You can ask for your data in a machine-readable format to transfer elsewhere.
Objection You can object to how we process your data in certain circumstances.
Restriction You can ask us to pause processing while a request is being resolved.

To exercise any of these rights, just email info@aircom.bg. We will respond within 30 days. If you feel we have not handled your data correctly, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP) of Bulgaria — cpdp.bg.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect your data — HTTPS throughout the site, restricted server access, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat data protection as a serious responsibility.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not retroactively change how we handle data you have already submitted without notifying you.
